Differentiator

Revalen Group was founded by specialist military intelligence operators, who applied their skills and experience to a wide range of humanitarian projects, including investigating atrocities, capacity development for governments and NGOs, and providing insights into global strategic threats.

We routinely leverage some of the most advanced commercial technology available, often identifying highly sensitive and effective capabilities well before they become mainstream.

We are partnered with world class cyber specialists, commercial data providers, legal specialists and engineers. We select teams that are agile, lean, and mission-driven.
