What this service is

Investigations are, by their nature, reliant on discovery. Every investigation therefore carries performance risk - can sufficient evidence be found to support a meaningful assessment or submission?

Our investigation teams minimise this risk through built-in decision points, allowing us to pivot at critical stages throughout the investigation.

We can provide an entire mission team, or bolster your existing team with capabilities such as:

Information collection and verification Analysis Technical support Project management Evidence preservation Liaison

What it does

Position your team as the go-to for high-value, high-impact investigations.

Complete complex investigations in weeks or months, not years.

Draw on multiple strands of privileged and open sources, including advanced technology to find unique insights.

Gain trust and confidence from your funders and delivery partners.

Prevent waste and save costs by triaging and adapting to the information environment.

Increase output and credibility quickly without extensive training or infrastructure changes.

Minimise trial and error through proven methodologies and a decision-led approach.

Avoid duplication of effort and retraumatisation through better coordination with partners.

Decision-led investigations

Discovery is uncertain. Your investigation approach should not be. Build in decision points early so time, funding, and effort are always focused on the highest impact.

What support looks like

  1. Discuss your project and desired outcomes with our team.
  2. Decide which investigation capabilities you require.
  3. We will then integrate a full or partial investigation team into your existing capabilities.
  4. You will be assigned a project manager and/or liaison, so you have a dedicated point of contact throughout.
  5. If required, we will facilitate access to specialist data or establish collection assets on your behalf.

Our support teams fit broadly into four categories, depending on the project, the information environment, and the outcomes you need to achieve.

Legal

  • Liaison with beneficiaries and prosecutors
  • Legal analysis and direction
  • Final submissions and commentary

Technical

  • Data acquisition and visualisation
  • Information management and preservation
  • Cyber and communication security

Operations

  • Training

Project Management

  • Quality assurance
  • Compliance
  • Measure of effect
